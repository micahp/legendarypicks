# Erratum for the pinned NHL event-data audit

This erratum is published separately so the original audit files and their
research-label verification chain remain unchanged.

## Canonical frame hash

Older prose in the NHL official-outcome documentation reports the SHA-256 of
`canonical_frame.csv` as beginning `336d8a8d`. The file used by the pinned
research-label and event audit chain hashes to:

`f417a1272b2bb4adff5757dae398dd1000dd2284f43b6ccb7dd28eaae99edf2b`

The value beginning `336d8a8d` is stale and must not be used as the source pin.
The full-frame game list was derived from the `f417a127…` frame and separately
hash-pinned at `ef02d94ff0ff5e494b8cdcb7eeca2f3e466e1fac1d30682c3e25cf4065ae55a8`.

## 300-game sample coordinate and shot-type coverage

The sample contains 34,508 PBP shot events. Both finite coordinates are present
for 34,506/34,508 (99.994%); two events lack a complete x/y pair. The overall
shot-type numerator is 26,037/34,508 (75.452%), but that aggregate combines two
different source behaviors:

| Event class | Events | Nonempty `shotType` | Coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Unblocked shots | 26,039 | 26,037 | 99.992% |
| Blocked shots | 8,469 | 0 | 0% |

Thus 8,471 sample shot events lack `shotType`: two unblocked shots and all
8,469 blocked shots. Blocked-shot types are absent in this sample and remain
unknown; no shot type or coordinate is filled by inference.
