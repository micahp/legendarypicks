For Legendary Picks, I would stop thinking about this as "what sports can I stream?" and instead think about "what live events make my predictions, props, and stats more valuable?"

That's a different optimization problem.

Right now Legendary Picks' moat isn't the video. It's that someone can watch a game while seeing:

live projections
player props
hit rates
historical game logs
AI insights
community picks

The stream is just the attention anchor.

I'd divide leagues into 4 buckets
Tier 1: Perfect fit

These have:

free streams
active betting communities
structured data
enough fans to matter
Call of Duty League
Valorant Challengers
Rocket League
PWHL
FIBA events
Ultimate Frisbee Association

These are where I'd spend engineering time.

Tier 2: Hidden gems

This is where I think there's real opportunity.

Instead of competing with ESPN, find sports nobody has built great products for.

Examples:

Chess
Poker tournaments
Drone Racing League
FIRST Robotics
Bass fishing tournaments
Disc golf
Pickleball
World Chase Tag
Strongman
CrossFit
Speedcubing
College esports

Many of these have:

YouTube streams
passionate communities
almost no prediction products
Tier 3: Creator competitions

These are incredibly underrated.

Examples:

Ludwig events
MrBeast competitions
Kai Cenat events
streamer tournaments
OTK
OfflineTV events

People already watch these on YouTube/Twitch.

Imagine:

Who wins?

Over/under eliminations

Fantasy scoring

No sportsbook is touching these.

Tier 4: Absolutely don't bother

The file already mentions MLS as an example that doesn't work because of exclusive rights, and that general principle applies broadly.

Skip:

NFL
NBA
MLB
NHL
MLS
UFC
Formula 1

The stream isn't your advantage there.

Notice only two are traditional sports.

That's intentional.

Those communities are underserved, have highly engaged fans, and often have fewer licensing barriers than the major North American leagues.

One idea that wasn't in your strategy note but I think fits Legendary Picks extremely well is becoming the home page for niche sports. If someone follows PWHL, CDL, Rocket League, or Disc Golf, they could come to Legendary Picks not just to watch, but to get live stats, projections, player comparisons, AI takes, and pick'em games all in one place. That combination is much harder to replicate than simply embedding a YouTube stream.
