## 4. Independent legality checks, with negative fixtures

- Checker: `legality_check.py`. It imports nothing from the engine. It restates ten rules from the record's own fields: blocked
  records are never complete; completion; points on drives equal the record's points; drive indices; clock monotone within a
  quarter; state continuity; downs and field position; timeout counts; possession at each drive boundary; and event legality.
- Result on all 300 engine games: 300 legal. Blocked games pass as blocked, not as complete.
- How this was reached: the first run flagged 12 games. Each flagged game was a checker error, in the possession rule, at a
  halftime or a missed field goal. The rule was corrected to read what the record states: the half-start kickoff at halftime, the
  kickoff after a score, the state after a punt or turnover, and the other side after a missed field goal. After the correction,
  the same 12 games are legal and the 300-seed count is 300 legal. Where the record gives no kickoff (a skipped post-score kick at
  a quarter end), the possession check is skipped, and the record does not say who has the ball there.
- Tests: `test_legality.py`, 14 tests, all OK. Two are positive: the frozen seed-1 record is legal, and a blocked record that is not
  complete is legal. Twelve are negative fixtures, mutated copies of seed 1, and each must fail the named check: a clock that rises
  inside a quarter; a quarter that falls; points that disagree with the drives; a drive index out of sequence; a timeout count of
  four; timeouts that do not reset at halftime; a blocked record marked complete; a down of five; a field position of 120; a punt
  that returns the ball to the punting side; a complete game ending above zero; and a touchdown recorded with three points.
- Limit: the checker and the rules are written by the same team, so the independence is of code and data, not of authorship.
  The rulebook text is not in the repository, so the rules are as stated in the checker, not as sourced.

## 5. Source-semantics audit

`docs/E2-SOURCE-SEMANTICS-AUDIT-2026-10-24.md`, updated in this commit.

- The audit now names every column the engine reads. It had not named eleven: the identifiers, `qtr`, `ydstogo`, `play_type`, the
  flags `interception`, `fumble_lost`, `sack`, `complete_pass` and `two_point_attempt`. A new section records each one with a
  measured count over 2022–2025 (188,282 rows). All identifiers are unique and non-null. Every completion, interception and
  sack is on a pass play.
- Corrected: the `season_type` row. The dev table has no `season_type` column. It holds weeks 1–18 only, so the window is the
  regular season. The `REG` filter is in the parquet kick builder, not in the dev table.
- Contradicted, and disclosed rather than corrected: the E1 sampling pool takes overtime rows. There are 1,214 quarter-5 rows, and
  the pool keys on down, distance and field, not on quarter. The engine never reaches overtime, but its pool contains those
  outcomes. Correcting it would change the frozen survey, so it is recorded here as a known effect.
