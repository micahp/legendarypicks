## 2. Second relay capture: `ingest_rotowire_snapshot.py` (uncommitted)

Micah declined browser pulls and didn't want the relay exhausted, and asked
for a cheap second pull: *"we can just add a second pull for rotowire, see
if the size or hash something is different and save it if it is."*

Shipped `backend/ingest_rotowire_snapshot.py` — one request per tick to
`lines.php`, writes `rotowire-<day>-snapHHMMSS.json.gz` **only when the
payload's content changed**:

- The comparison is a **content digest** (markets, entities, projections,
  per-book line/over/under, hitRates), NOT raw bytes — the relay re-stamps
  `lineTime` on unchanged lines (measured: 10 lines re-stamped within two
  minutes, zero content change), and a raw-byte hash would store dozens of
  identical snapshots per day.
- `latest_hash()` picks the newest capture **by mtime, not filename sort** —
  alphabetical order ranks the daily `rotowire-<day>.json.gz` AFTER same-day
  snapshots (`-` < `.`), which made the midnight archive look like the
  newest capture forever and produced a "CHANGED" verdict every run.
- `--report` lists the day's captures; `--dry-run` fetches and compares
  without writing.
- Verified: change → writes; identical → one request, no write.
- **NOT on a timer yet.** A candidate cadence is alongside the props timer
  (30min). `monitor_rotowire_soccer.py` already probes the relay every 6h
  (00/06/12/18:07) for the "does the board carry MLS" question; the snapshot
  adds full-payload capture between the probe reads.

**Deletion note:** two bug-generated test snapshots
(`snap072920/072921.json.gz`, written by the pre-fix comparison) were
deleted to keep the archive clean — flagged to Micah at the time; ask before

## 4. Props odds taxonomy (committed `8ffd6dd`, doc)

`docs/PROPS-ODDS-TAXONOMY.md` — measured from the dev DB, no network:

- **Real sportsbook odds**: `bovada` (69 distinct), direct `underdog` (187),
  all relay `-sb` books, `rotowire:sleeper` (60). **Placeholders**:
  `rotowire:prizepicks` / `rotowire:underdog` (-137 constant), direct
  `prizepicks*` (odds NULL). `-sb` in a relay source name means sportsbook.
- **Real-vs-real disagreement**: 29,554 pairs on identical
  game+player+market+line+side; median odds delta 17; 21.2% flip
  favorite/underdog. Mostly MLB (29,028); NFL is tight (median 5-12).
- **Coverage map**: Bovada is the only real source for tennis, WC, lcup,
  `card_shown`, `win_by_decision`, and its MLB combos
  (`total_doubles`, `total_hits,_runs_and_rbis`, `total_pitcher_walks`).
  Bovada has ZERO NFL player props and zero MLB hitting markets (hits, runs,
  rbis, hits_runs_rbis, walks) — the `-sb`/sleeper books cover those.
- **Placeholder-only markets** (why placeholders keep being ingested): MLS's
  7 depth markets entirely (passes_attempted, saves, shots, SOT, clearances,
  crosses, chances_created) and NFL special teams/depth (kicking_points,
  field_goals_made, extra_points_made, pass_attempts, pass_completions,
  rush_attempts, sacks, targets, rushing_touchdowns,
  rushing_receiving_touchdowns). 56.6% of placeholder line-slots have no
  real book on that line.
- **Underdog direct vs relay underdog**: ZERO overlap (direct = UFC only;
  relay = NFL/MLB). No dedupe decision needed.
- **Bovada's line is explicit**: `price.handicap` → `props.line`
  (`parsers.py:467`); nothing is inferred except the yes/no convention
  (goalscorer → `line=0.5 over`). Bovada carries real unders (28,591);
  over-only rows are the yes/no markets where no under exists as a bet.
- **The ~5% NULL-odds Bovada rows**: ALL 5,204 are from 2026-06-15..24, pre-
  M6 (before odds capture landed). Since July 1: zero. Keep them — Micah
  said don't delete; they are line-only legacy rows.
- **Kalshi (checked live, per Micah)**: series exist for the no-odds markets
  (KXNFLSACK, KXNFLPASSATT/COMP, KXNFLRSHATT, KXNFLGAMEFG) but have **zero
  events posted** — dead. MLS: no player series at all (34 KXMLS series, all
  team-level; confirms the 08-25 audit). Where Kalshi IS live (NFL milestone
  props: REC/RSHYDS/RECYDS/PASSYDS/PASSTDS/ANYTD), markets carry an explicit
  `floor_strike` line + yes-probability pricing (cents), one-sided — a
  probability source for the M7 EV/calibration work, NOT an odds source for
  the board. Candidate ingest design if wanted later.

## 5. Default-line rule on the props board (committed `5a711b0`)

Micah's spec: *"it's both"* — Bovada AND the other books. Where they collide
on the same prop, keep Bovada (most liquidity); take the other books' props
Bovada doesn't have; no duplicate props on the board. The known semantic
twins are the same prop under different keys: `total_hits,_runs_and_rbis` ≡
`hits_runs_rbis` (2,176 true duplicate pairs) and `total_doubles` ≡
`doubles` (833). Bovada has the wider line selection in the identical-key
collisions (strikeouts 8 lines vs relay 6; outs 13 vs 4). The three combo
markets to drop entirely: `total_touchdowns`, `passing_rushing_yards`,
`rushing_receiving_yards` — **not yet dropped** (see Open).
