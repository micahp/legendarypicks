Read `docs/PROPS-SOURCES.md` alongside this; it is the durable reference and
this is the day's narrative.

## The one thing to carry forward

**Every gap found today was our own bug, and the instinct to go find a new
provider was wrong every time.** The day started with "should we probe
PrizePicks or Sleeper or DraftKings" and ended having restored three lanes from
publishers that were never blocking us. Bovada served 3,147 MLS props to this
datacenter IP on the first correct request.

The shape they shared: **HTTP 200, well-formed response, short list.** An
out-of-season sport and a truncated response are indistinguishable from outside,
so nothing raised and every instrument read healthy while three lanes were dead.

## 1. Bovada's bare coupon path truncates every league

Any query parameter defeats it. Same minute:

```
soccer/north-america/united-states/mls   bare  0 events    lang=en 15
baseball/mlb                             bare 15 events    param   27
```

So MLS read as "Bovada does not carry this league" while it carried the whole
13-fixture slate, and **MLB has been ingesting roughly half its fixtures** for
an unknown period. Not re-measured for the other leagues yet.

Use `lang=en`. I tried `marketFilterId=def` first and it is a trap: it is
Bovada's default market *filter*, returns Game Lines only, and strips every
player prop. Caught before committing, by checking what the parameter returned
rather than assuming a parameter named "default" was neutral.

## 2. MLS was removed on a premise that stopped being true

On 2026-08-17 MLS came out of `LEAGUES` because Bovada prices 2 of the eleven
markets while the relay prices 7, and the stated reason was that a second book
writing goals and assists would mean **two sources disagreeing**.

There was no disagreement to create: the relay writes **zero** goals and zero
assists. Tonight's slate was 428 props across 8 markets, every row
`rotowire:prizepicks`, not one goalscorer line. The newest goals row in the
entire database was 2026-08-17, the day of the removal. The product shipped a
soccer league with no goalscorer market for 19 days.

Restored. Prod now carries goals 1716, assists 390, first_goal_scorer 521,
goal_or_assist 404, on top of the relay's eight. 8 markets to 12, all priced.

## 3. Tennis died mid-Slam while we were trading it

`tennis/atp` and `tennis/wta` return 200 with zero events. Bovada files tennis
under the tournament: `tennis/us-open/men-s-singles`. The bare `tennis` path
carries 102 events including today's matches.

Restored to prod: ATP 90 of 90 resolved, WTA 85 of 92, 24 matches.

**Still open and valuable:** the coupon carries Player Props, Service Game
Props, Break Props and Ace/Double Fault Props. We parse only `match_winner`,
`total_games` and `win_a_set`. Service Game and Break props are precisely the
hold-and-break data the tennis reprice model needs, sitting in a payload we
already fetch.

## 4. The backoff turned three outages into six

The adaptive backoff rests a league after three empty runs. Truncated runs look
empty, so it slept them: **atp 29 empty runs, wc 63, wta 28, lcup 12, nfl 4**
with the NFL season opening this week. Even after the truncation was fixed the
leagues stayed asleep, and clearing the counters by hand was required.

A backoff that cannot distinguish "the publisher has nothing" from "we asked
wrong" will re-suppress every lane we fix. Unresolved.

## 5. Residential IP is not the lever, and PrizePicks is not the answer

The X outage was solved by a residential pull, so the obvious move was to point
the same machine at PrizePicks. It 403s a **verified residential IP** (Charter,
Austin) using curl with matching headers. Not an IP-range block: a logged-in
browser is served, scripted clients are not. The home-PC session was right to
stop rather than start rotating headers.

And PrizePicks would not have satisfied the requirement anyway. Micah's
requirement is provenance, all the props, **and odds**. PrizePicks props are
**0% priced** — demon and goblin are payout multipliers, not prices. It fills
market coverage and cannot fill the odds column.

## 6. The X lanes, both repos

Newsletter corpus refilled through the residential git drop: geoppls +39,
innovativehype +21, newest now 2026-09-04 against 2026-08-27. Media and links
added to the drop schema, because X blocks this box on the tweet surface but
**not on its media CDN** — verified, pbs.twimg.com serves 200 at `name=orig`
and video.twimg.com serves a 1080p playlist. Only URLs cross; bytes are fetched
here by `scripts/fetch_media.py`.

LP's news X lane now falls back to a residential drop when every mirror is dead,
with `collect_x_desktop()`. A live mirror always wins, because this lane's value
is perishable. The drop gives canonical `x.com/<handle>/status/<id>` permalinks,
better provenance than the mirror links it replaces.

A freshness alarm that does not depend on the transport it watches now runs on
cron: local files only, exits 1 naming any lane past 48h. Its absence is why a
dead lane went unnoticed for nine days.
