## 4. Known E2 defects, from my own audit (not yet fixed)

1. EP and two-point rates derive from `success`, which may be EPA-based (item 1).
2. Missed field goal gives possession at `100 - kick_yl`. Rule 11-4-2, as stated in the review: possession at the kick spot, or at the receiving team's own 20 when the kick spot is inside the 20. Blocked and touched kicks are not modelled, and no test covers the boundary.
3. `drive_index`: E1 state dictionaries carry `drive_index: 1` on every play; the game-level index is not written into `state_before` or `state_after`.
4. Toss: the winner is always team `A` (`CoinTossLedger('A', 'B', ...)`). Both winner identities and both choices are not sampled or tested together.
5. Matrix rows "Q1->Q2 change of ends", "Q3->Q4 change of ends" and "Touchdown ... post-score kickoff" return PASS while they depend on a provisional practice kick. They should be PROVISIONAL.
6. Overtime and thin cells: blocked, and labelled as blocked, but the survey does not yet say per seed which games are *not complete* and why.
