The workload arm disabled both the Dirichlet layer and its dependent fixed-share adapter, and is a combined intervention. Both arms used the five ordinary blocker fixes. The raw smoke output and reports are on the fast host at `/root/ot-sim-out/slc-matrix-ari-sea-8df4fc43/`.

A separate 50-seed evaluation at commit `eb936b6e` used the same matchup, ordinary fixes, RNG v2 and the fast host's read-only `picks.dev.db`. Both intervention reports recorded matching streams for all 50 seeds, but neither is a complete 50-game comparison. The score-clock arm has 48 reconciled pairs: control seed 18 was refused by the validator, and ablation seed 28 was blocked by `no_supported_scrimmage_intent_in_cell`. The workload arm also has 48: control seed 18 and ablation seed 31 were refused. The matrix preserves all 50 requested seed rows and labels both full comparisons invalid.

The saved seed 18 record ties on a touchdown and try at regulation expiry; a later validator correction accepts it. The saved seed 31 record misses a field goal as the first half expires and then receives the second-half kickoff; a separate later correction accepts it. Both complete saved records are retained as test fixtures. Those corrections were not used by the 50-seed run, and neither failure is reclassified in these results. The score-clock seed 28 is a source-coverage guard, not one of the validator cases; the earlier run saved its transition envelope but not a complete blocked record. Later code captures incomplete records when one exists.

The detailed [score-clock review](score_clock_playcalling.json) and [combined workload review](dirichlet_player_target_carry_shares.json) were postprocessed from the original ledgers and box scores using reporting code at `1f239e12`. They retain all seed statuses, set score deltas for invalid pairs to null, report only reconciled complete box-score pairs in the distributions, and add paired player carry and target shares. Carry share is player carries divided by team rush attempts; target share is player targets divided by team modeled targets. A pair is omitted when either denominator is zero or unavailable. The engine does not model throwaways or spikes, so modeled targets equal modeled pass attempts. Raw original reports, worker outputs, ledgers, box scores, and failure sidecars remain unchanged on the fast host at `/root/ot-sim-out/slc-matrix-ari-sea-eb936b6e-50/`; each review records original report and artifact hashes.

For descriptive context only, across the 48 completed score-clock pairs, disabling score-clock changed ARI mean points from 25.44 to 21.48 and SEA mean points from 25.04 to 23.25. In the 48 completed combined-workload pairs, mean modeled carry share for SEA's Zach Charbonnet changed from 0.762 to 0.359, and mean modeled target share for ARI's Michael Wilson from 0.427 to 0.205. These are selected observations in one matchup, under different exclusion sets, not measured historical accuracy, evidence of statistical significance, or forecasts.

Fallback counts cover all 50 seeds with playcall traces, including incomplete games. The control recorded 22,418 fallback events, the score-clock arm 21,009, and the workload arm 22,692. Counts are events, not unique plays; 20,038, 18,764, and 19,755 respectively were missing decision-category matches. These numbers demonstrate coverage instrumentation, not the accuracy impact of the fallbacks.
