## Decision and limits

After the preregistered out-of-game additive correction, P1 negative-error persistence remains above its within-start permutation null for at least one candidate with a positive whole-game 95% gap interval. This is descriptive evidence in ten development games only; it does not establish pair-intent causality or justify building labels for adoption.

These ten inspected games remain development data. The tracking feed lacks a documented subsecond timebase; Image Id is ordinal. Player identities can conflict, cross-game athlete identity is unverified, event-to-tracking alignment failed its prior gate, and puck Z is not continuous flight measurement. The present analysis does not establish physical velocity, reaction timing, possession, collision mechanics, or a validated NHL simulator. Independent games with verified timing and identity evidence are required for any adoption claim.
