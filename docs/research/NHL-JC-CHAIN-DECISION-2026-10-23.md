## What would unblock, and what it is not

An identity-bearing feed would need to provide persistent athlete IDs, canonical game IDs, and a documented direct mapping to the tracking IDs. That is a limit on this one test, not on the ten games or the movement work: questions that do not require cross-game identity remain open and can proceed.
