# SLC play-calling ablation specification M3 — 2026-11-02

Status: frozen research protocol. This spec authorizes no production/default change and no run by itself. Its first freeze commit and this file's SHA-256 are recorded in `PLAYCALL-ABLATION-SPEC-M3-FREEZE-RECORD-2026-11-02.md` after the freeze commit is created.

## 1. Question and scope

Test whether SLC's team opportunity structure is better explained by drive/possession volume or by run/dropback choice. Keep these mechanisms separate before changing individual player allocation. Do not add post-hoc player workload caps or use simulated injuries to limit workload.

M3 arms test team opportunities only. Personnel eligibility, player-share changes, defensive-response changes, fatigue, injuries, and coach policy are separate later work. The 2022–2025 personnel table is frozen here as a baseline input; this spec does not alter player eligibility. Coach policy remains deferred until a dated team/coach join is verified under gate 4.

## 2. Canonical observed definitions and metric denominators

Canonical source: `docs/research/playcall-baseline-2022-2025/README.md`, generated by `build_baseline.py` from the pinned nflverse files below. The source write-up is not canonical.

- **Population:** regular-season PBP rows, seasons 2022–2025, with `posteam` present.
- **Scrimmage plays:** `play_type` in `{run, pass}`, excluding `qb_kneel` and `qb_spike`.
- **Run outcome:** `play_type == run`. **Dropback outcome:** `play_type == pass`, including sacks. These are the only rows in the play-call log-loss and Brier denominators. Run is the positive class.
- **Drives:** distinct `(game_id, drive)` within a team that contain at least one scrimmage play. **Plays per drive:** scrimmage-play count divided by that drive count.
- **Per-game counts/rates:** divide the team's season count by distinct regular-season `game_id` values for that team. The baseline support unit is one team-season; the 2022–2025 team table has 32 teams per season.
- **Carries:** player-attributed run plays grouped by `rusher_player_name`. **Targets:** dropbacks grouped by non-null `receiver_player_name`; sacks have no receiver. **Season share:** player opportunities divided by that team's total of the same opportunity type in the same season.
- **Personnel:** `personnel_code` is the count of RBs followed by the count of TEs in `offense_personnel`, matching `formation_layer.personnel_code`. The per-season/team table reports plays and share; it is a package-frequency baseline, not an on-field player-ID join.

For each held-out play, log loss is `-[y log(p) + (1-y) log(1-p)]` and Brier is `(p-y)^2`; each is averaged over eligible play rows. Arm differences are candidate minus A0. The 95% paired interval resamples whole `game_id` clusters, retaining paired arm losses; use the existing method in `playcall_uncertainty.py` (2,000 replicates, seed 20261028, percentile interval). The prior script's fold choices are not reused.

For team opportunity counts, calculate each team's regular-season per-game rate for runs, dropbacks, scrimmage plays, and drives; calculate plays per drive as defined above. For each held-out season and metric, simulated rates are averaged over the matched game/seed runs and compared with the observed team-season rate. Absolute bias is the absolute difference for each team-season; report all 32 team-season cells and the equal-weight mean absolute bias for each metric. Apply D3's 5% relative-worsening limit separately to each opportunity metric targeted by an arm. If A0's mean absolute bias is zero, the relative comparison is undefined and that gate is inconclusive.

For any later player-share arm, a player share is player opportunities divided by same-type team opportunities within that team-game. Preserve zero-opportunity counts for eligible players. Report RB and WR separately. A variance ratio is candidate predicted share variance divided by observed share variance over supported player-team units; units need at least two scored team-games for a sample variance. Report support and uncertainty. This WP1 spec does not fit or score a player-share arm; the D3 share gates are binding for any later such arm, and insufficient cohort support is inconclusive.

## 4. Arms

Every arm uses the same frozen game list, source inputs, snapshots, seed IDs, and paired random streams. Preserve blocked and failed seeds; do not silently drop a seed from one arm.

| Arm | Frozen role | Single mechanism changed |
|---|---|---|
| A0 | Control: current `v1_full_stack` and score-clock policy. `playcall_playbook` stays enabled as resolved by `slc_config.V1_OPTIONS`; do not edit the profile. | None. |
| A1 | Drive/possession-volume alternative. | Drive and possession volume only; run/dropback choice remains A0. |
| A2 | Situation-conditioned run/dropback alternative. | Run/dropback choice only, conditioned on down, distance, field position, score, and clock; drive-volume mechanism remains A0. |
| A3 | Optional interaction arm, considered only after A1 and A2 have complete development-fold results. | A1 and A2 together. |

The functional forms for A1 and A2 are not specified in the cited records. Before any fit, write and commit one form for each arm, identify its inputs and fallbacks, and freeze it for development folds A and B. A3 must likewise be specified before it is run. No form may be selected using 2025 or 2026 values.

## 5. Advance gates

Apply the owner-approved thresholds below; these are engineering decision rules, not claims of optimality.

**Play-calling alternatives:**

1. Candidate run/dropback log loss improves by at least 0.001 absolute over the corresponding A0 baseline.
2. The paired, game-clustered 95% confidence interval supports improvement (candidate-minus-A0 interval is below zero).
3. Run/dropback Brier score increases by no more than 0.001 absolute over A0.

**Any later player-share or participation alternative:**

1. RB and WR share-variance ratios are within 0.80–1.20 of observed for adequately supported cohorts.
2. Absolute variance-ratio error falls by at least 10% versus A0.
3. Team opportunity-count absolute bias does not worsen by more than 5% relative to A0.

**All arms:** zero new football-legality or accounting failures. Report effect sizes, paired game-cluster bootstrap intervals, coverage, zero rates, P95/P99 behavior, subgroup regressions, and support counts. Account for multiple candidate comparisons; the correction procedure must be fixed before final 2025 evaluation and cannot be selected from 2025 results. Insufficient support is inconclusive, not a pass. Report rare tails; do not impose individual-player ceilings. Passing the retrospective gates qualifies an arm for prospective validation and does not adopt it into production.
