## Model added

### Team-conditioned outcomes

Pregame team scoring means use the frozen B3 Elo model. Expected SOG combines prior player shot
rates and prior goalie shots-against rates. Donor periods are selected with a tempered likelihood
on the matchup's per-period team SOG priors. On-target opportunities are resampled as misses,
blocks, shots on goal, saves, or goals. Goal probability is conditioned on the training-derived
shot type/distance bucket, simulated shooter finishing prior, sampled goalie evidence, and a
pregame team-level conversion offset. Every goal remains the outcome of a generated shot event.

### Player and goalie attribution

The pregame roster snapshot supplies eligible NHL player IDs. Prior player evidence, with
population shrinkage, weights shooter, faceoff, penalty, hit, and assist participation. Goalie
plans are uncertain samples, with conditional shot conversion adjusted by the sampled goalie’s
prior save evidence. Simulated skater and goalie totals are reconciled against the generated
event stream.

### Shift and personnel ledger

Verified historical shift intervals are remapped to the frozen pregame roster by position and
usage rank. The remapped intervals produce an explicit TOI ledger, on-ice event participants,
and goalie changes at supported shift boundaries. Malformed intervals, unresolved identities,
overlaps after remapping, missing lineup coverage, and invalid personnel counts are rejected and
counted; source hashes or PBP/box-score eligibility failures remain fatal. No tracking-derived
physical speed or reaction time is used.

### Donor data quarantine

The Monte Carlo output reports rejected donor periods by reason and caps example records at 20.
There were 190 rejected donor periods for COL–NSH and 168 for MIN–VAN; 3,095 and 3,147 shift rows
from otherwise accepted donor sources, respectively, were quarantined. Main reasons included
overlapping intervals, invalid personnel counts, invalid shift boundaries, and missing goalie
coverage. These counts describe this instrument, the sampled 2019–2020 donor periods, and the
shift manifest—not the completeness of all NHL shift charts. The high quarantine volume is a
material limitation on deployment realism and should be reduced only by validating the source,
not by relaxing eligibility rules.

## Findings and limitations

This is a working research prototype, not the full state-conditioned event process in the
specification and not an adoption candidate. Team IDs now affect goal and SOG priors, donor-period
selection, and regulation 5v5 shot conversion. The 5v5 shot process converts generated attempt
events into misses, blocks, SOG, saves, and goals; other strength states still retain donor
template outcomes. Event clocks, most event ordering, penalty transitions, and special-team
sequences still come from historical period templates. The generator does not yet recompute every
competing event hazard from simulated score, possession, zone, penalties, and clock. Possession
and unobserved passing/tactical actions remain latent, and there is not yet a learned team tactical
policy.

Player participation, shot/goal/assist attribution, goalie save adjustment, and TOI are simulated
from prior evidence with population shrinkage and roster eligibility. They are not independently
validated player forecasts. The shift sequence is a remapped historical donor, not an independently
learned substitution policy. Roster membership is known only at the frozen roster-snapshot level;
historical pregame-eligible lineup and confirmed starter coverage are not established for unbiased
player/goalie evaluation. Fatigue, player location-dependent decisions, team tactics, late-game
goalie-pull policy, and physical motion are not modeled as causal policies. The target-night output
must not be used as evidence that these features improve prediction accuracy.

No historical player-level adoption evaluation is claimed. The 2021 sample remains development
data, and 2022 is previously exposed. A later-season or prospective evaluation with genuinely
as-of roster/lineup/goalie inputs is still required before adoption. The Gridiron event viewer
and physical integration remain separate future work.

## Decision

Keep v0.1 frozen and retain v0.2 alpha2 as a research-only prototype. These slate samples show
that team IDs, eligible roster identities, uncertain goalie evidence, and remapped shifts can
influence reproducible games with reconciled score, shot, player, goalie, and TOI records. They do
not establish predictive superiority, validated player deployment, or a publishable pregame pick.
