# 2025 official inactive adjudication: ACT conflicts and unresolved identities

This note audits the 21 exact-key official inactive identities that join to an `ACT` row in the 2025 weekly roster, and all 10 unresolved official inactive identities. The match key is exact `(game_type, week, team, GSIS ID)`; no roster-based identity resolution was used. The roster-row fields below are from `scratchpad/avail/rosters/roster_weekly_2025.parquet`.

`nfl_hist_injuries.date_modified` is NULL for all 6,068 2025 rows. Therefore, 2025 cannot support any timing analysis.

## ACT conflicts

Seventeen of the 21 records are corroborated as inactive by an NFL gamebook's `Not Active` section or an official team inactive announcement. Four are contradicted by game-day evidence and are corrected in a new label version by removing those four inactive rows: Damone Clark (REG W5 DAL), Harold Landry (REG W13 NE), and Logan Lee (REG W16 and W17 PIT).

The original JSONL and retrospective report remain frozen and unchanged. The versioned [JSONL](official_inactives_2025_full_v2.jsonl) contains 3,600 rows after those four removals; its SHA-256 is `cb326297258f513b2097110f30c3a8398536fe064989ef626ee8cbf4eb05995a`. The original published validation totals and rates remain as reported against the frozen NFL.com labels; no recalculated rate report was produced.
