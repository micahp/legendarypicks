# 2022 movement patterns, first slice: weeks 1–9 (2026-10-24)

What this is: the 2022 observed movement of every matched play, reduced to per-player rows and then to patterns by side,
position, play kind and down. It is the movement foundation the 2026 generator would adapt. It is not a simulation, it does
not use 2026 players, and it has no formation, personnel or result dimension yet.

## Scope and checks

- Plays: the 15,765 matched plays of the join (`bdb2022-join-pairs-2026-10-24.csv`), weeks 1–9.
- Player-play rows: 346,830. Every play has 22 players, 11 on each side, checked across all nine weeks.
- Frame rate: the frame id steps by 1 and the time stamps by 0.1 s on all 397,645 consecutive pairs sampled from week 1.
- Window: from the `SNAP` frame to six seconds after it (60 frames). No play was skipped: every play had a snap frame and a
  ball frame at the snap.
- Tracking inputs (hashes from `/root/nfl-tracking-data/bdb2025/SHA256SUMS.txt`): `tracking_week_1.csv`
  `dc47c829…`, `_2` `d735b37f…`, `_3` `db2965ad…`, `_4` `2e931bc9…`, `_5` `1c101365…`, `_6` `68469299…`, `_7` `29fb1b47…`,
  `_8` `b27824f4…`, `_9` `bb51989c…`.

## Method

- Per player: alignment at the snap, relative to the ball frame. Depth is along the play direction (`+1` when
  `playDirection` is `right`), lateral is the y offset.
- Speed in each one-second bin after the snap (six bins), taken as the median of the frames in that bin. Peak speed and peak
  acceleration in the window. Distance covered in the first three seconds, summed from the frame distances.
- Side: offense when the player's club is the possession team. Both use BDB abbreviations.
- Play kind: `isDropback` is `TRUE` for a pass (dropbacks include sacks and scrambles) and `FALSE` for a run. `plays.csv`
  writes these as `TRUE` and `FALSE`.
- Position: the BDB label from `players.csv`, not normalised.
- Cells: side × position × play kind × down, reported with at least 30 player-play rows. Medians of observed values; no
  intervals are reported.

Correction made during this slice: the first run matched `True` and missed `TRUE`, so every cell was labelled `run`. The
extraction was rerun with the corrected match, and the pass and run cells are reported from that rerun.

per-player rows are regenerated by the script and are not committed.

## Limits

- Observed 2022 movement only. Nothing here is adapted to the 2026 players' speed, acceleration or ratings. That adaptation
  is the next step and needs the player snapshot.
- Not split by formation, personnel or result yet. The per-player rows carry the play key, so those dimensions can be added
  by joining `plays.csv` (`offenseFormation`, `yardsGained`, `passResult`) without re-reading the tracking.
- The depth sign follows the play direction. It is not checked against a formation chart. The signs in the table are the
  expected ones (defenders ahead of the ball, offensive skill players behind it), but this is not a validation.
- Punts and field goals are not in the joined set (BDB has none), so no special-teams movement is reported.
- Ball trajectories are used only as the snap anchor.
