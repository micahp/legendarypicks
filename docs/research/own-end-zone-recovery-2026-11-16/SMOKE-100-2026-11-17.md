## Caveats

1. The effective pool is 2025 only. The output declares a 2022 to 2025 window. Its reference-pool scope lists eligible play seasons of [2025]. The 2022 to 2024 rows are not in the rebuilt history. This is the main reason the run cannot be compared with V1.6.
2. Every play carries `field.source = authored_demo_orientation`. The field positions may not be oriented from real data. This label needs an audit before any field-position claim.
3. The provenance block records the penalty model by file hash. It also records the canonical model hash in the proposals. Both are present and they agree.
4. The reconstructed host runtime is verified by content only. The host produced the provenance record, so this check is not independent of the host.
