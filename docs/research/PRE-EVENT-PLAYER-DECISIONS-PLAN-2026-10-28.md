# Plan: pre-event player decisions, 2026-10-28

Goal: the participants of a play are chosen before its outcome is drawn, so player statistics are generated by the same
decisions that create the team's events. Today, `game_stats.py` assigns players after the play, so player lines are
post-hoc attribution, not a decision model.

## Order of decisions in one play

1. State: team, down, distance, field, score, quarter, clock, timeouts (from the play-calling layer).
2. Action: run, dropback, punt, field goal, go for it (play-calling layer, team-tendency and context priors).
3. Participants, before the outcome:
   - QB identity from the cutoff-safe depth chart and usage (the QB must be on the field on a dropback or a designed run).
   - Rusher on a designed run from cutoff-safe position usage.
   - Target on a dropback from cutoff-safe receiver usage, conditioned on the team's target distribution.
4. Dropback branch: sack, scramble or throw, from the QB's sack and scramble rates in the situation.
5. Throw branch: completion, yards and interception conditioned on the target and the situation, from observed plays.
6. Statistical credits come from the drawn event and its participants only (passer, rusher, receiver, yards, touchdown,
   interception, fumble), so every stat line reconciles to the drive and the score.

## Data each step needs (cutoff-safe)

| Step | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| QB identity | depth chart before cutoff; game logs before cutoff | historical depth is prior-week, publication unverified |
| Rusher and target usage | `player_game_logs` 2010-2025 and position usage (needs cutoff) | position usage is not cutoff-safe yet |
| Snap workload | `nfl_hist_snap_counts` 2013-2025 | not built into the engine |
| Completion, yards, interception | nfl_pbp 2010-2025, cutoff-filtered | observation builder built; pass-outcome model not yet |
| Sack, scramble | nfl_pbp `sack`, `qb_scramble` | observation builder carries both flags |

## Tests the step must pass before it counts

- Each participant is chosen before the event and never reads the drawn outcome.
- Stat lines sum to the drive and score (complete-game reconciliation).
- A later game cannot change an earlier participant choice.
- Missing participants (no depth row, no usage) fall back to the team-level role, and the fallback is logged, never silent.
- S0 remains unchanged.

## Not in this plan

- NGS separation and other receiving metrics stay out of v1 (coverage is 2024 to 2025 and game-level).
- FTN and participation features join as separate layers with their own windows, and never gate a play.
