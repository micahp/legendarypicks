# Madden 64 statistical simulation study

Status: **in progress**, 2026-11-24. One CPU-controlled exhibition has reached the end of game; no verified event recorder or statistical pilot has been captured. All proposed Madden statistical effects remain hypotheses. This document is a research protocol and evidence ledger, not a statistical results report.

## Gate 0: reproducibility and recorder

1. Locate the previously supplied authorized USA Rev 0 image outside the repository and verify SHA-256 `39f25dad3af4782a909bf58d7127acae700ea7c6f17c48d39ab76fe331735d6f`. Record emulator, core, plugins, configuration and image hash; keep image, derived proprietary assets and save states outside git.
2. Start exhibition with both controllers centered, fixed teams and options. Observe complete autonomous play and any prompts through the final box score. Record screen and input sequence. Distinguish this from attract mode and season simulation.
3. Save a start state; repeat with identical inputs and compare event sequences and final state. Locate RNG state through source and runtime evidence before changing it. Demonstrate distinct trajectories for candidate initial states and exact replay for each. Menu delays alone do not prove independent seeds.
4. Identify clock, teams, score, down, distance, spot, play and possession transitions by source inspection plus live changes. Record raw frame, raw values, decoded values, address provenance and confidence. Capture plays, scores, penalties, kicks, turnovers, calls, players and interactions only as semantics are verified; unknown stays null. Save pre/post state and detect missed transitions. A visible scoreboard is an independent oracle, not a substitute for events.
5. Reconcile both teams' scores and every available displayed team statistic against the event aggregation, including scoring attribution, accepted penalties, sacks, kneels, tries, returns, turnovers and overtime. Capture screenshots and explicit counterexamples. Resolve discrepancies before batch collection. State fields not displayed require independent frame-by-frame observations. Log each run's state hash, seed provenance, config, teams, quarter length, duration, version and failure reason.

Stage 0 target is 35 complete games only after the single-game gates pass. Thirty-five games cannot also provide ten independent games at each of four quarter lengths: stage 1 requires at least 40 games, with any overlap explicitly enumerated. No game is counted twice as independent.

## Experimental protocol

**A: realism.** Compare per team-game points, touchdowns, made/attempted field goals, plays, yards, sacks, interceptions, lost fumbles, punts, accepted penalties, drives, completion percentage, passing yards per attempt, rush yards per carry, third-down conversions, possession duration and scoring-drive percentage to documented era NFL definitions. Show totals and per-opportunity rates, numerator/denominator and uncertainty. Separate regulation and overtime; identify offensive versus defensive/special-teams scores. Report distribution and individual games alongside means. NFL and Madden populations, team selections and play definitions may differ.

**B: funnel.** For every offensive drive record start field position; scrimmage plays and elapsed clock; yards and explosives; third-down attempts and conversions; red-zone entries (first offensive snap inside opponent 20, with alternate definition documented); field-goal range using distance bands rather than an assumed cutoff; touchdowns and field goals. Report each stage per drive and conditional conversion using eligible drives as denominators. Drives can enter multiple states and end by half, turnover, kick or score: preserve competing outcomes, defensive scores and missing values. Compare corresponding CEG and era NFL populations only where the same definition is available. No causal compensation or intent follows merely from two opposing aggregate errors.

**C: duration.** Ten independent games at each manual-listed 3, 5, 10 and 15 minute setting, fixed settings and matched mirrored matchups where feasible. Report regulation plays and points per game-clock minute, yards/play, drives/game, snap intervals split by clock-running/stopped states, fourth-down decision opportunities and timeouts. Distinguish emulator frames, real elapsed time and game clock. Compare efficiency with uncertainty; a duration interaction is evidence of changed outcomes, not by itself proof of changed physics.

**D: teams.** Establish team identity and offense/defense ratings from observed setup; select equal, offensive/defensive mismatches, strong/strong and strong/weak pairs. Mirror home and away, use independent initial states, and report game counts, mean margin, standard deviation, win/tie share, points/drive and event rates. Pair comparable starts only if seed behavior is proven. Home-order differences are estimates, not isolated effects if other settings change.

**E: ratings.** Gate on verified named attribute, player identity, runtime mapping and controlled write with unchanged other fields. For QB accuracy/awareness, RB speed/break tackle, catching, blocking, tackling and kicking, compare multiple levels and matched control states. Track downstream events and confidence intervals; restore state and check player selection and transformed runtime values. Do not label a raw byte from a single correlation.

**F: situations.** Create and validate save states for third-and-1/15, fourth-and-2 at midfield, goal-to-go at 2, first-and-10 at midfield, late trailing/leading by seven, backed up at 1, and same offensive play against varied coverage. Record all preconditions, clock, score, field orientation, calls, RNG provenance and outcomes; compare repeated independent starts and replicate exact states. State construction and initial conditions can change AI behavior and must be checked visually and in RAM.

## Analysis and release gates

Predeclare selections and thresholds before outcomes. Use game as the sampling unit for uncertainty (cluster drives/plays by game), show counts and intervals appropriate to pilot size, and avoid causal claims from observational comparisons. Decompose points per drive by scoring type and opportunities, and investigate interactions with source traces or controlled interventions. Preserve raw logs, exclusions, configuration, checksums and analysis version. Maintain separate evidence labels: manual, source-verified mechanism, emulator observation, inference and hypothesis. A result is not validated until an independent screen or controlled observation can falsify its recorder mapping.

## Static recorder leads (unverified at runtime)

The pinned source's matched [roster_stat_process](https://github.com/TheGh0stShip/Madden-64/blob/3f79881ce369abbba504c87eb3a6520af53c1cbf/src/code/roster_stat_process.c) reads 16-bit team-indexed values at `D_80082D22` for most statistic indices when `D_8006B9D8 == 0`, and computes several indices from player rows or the opposite team. In the other mode it reads through a different pointer. The matched [team_stat_value_get](https://github.com/TheGh0stShip/Madden-64/blob/3f79881ce369abbba504c87eb3a6520af53c1cbf/src/code/team_stat_value_get.c) reads player-indexed signed 16-bit statistics under one mode, with other accessors in another. These are candidates for a box-score oracle, not yet named statistics or demonstrated live counters. Different accessor paths, array indexing, lifetime, update timing and any overlapping declarations must be resolved with screen comparisons before recording them as football facts.

The matched [random_scale_u16](https://github.com/TheGh0stShip/Madden-64/blob/3f79881ce369abbba504c87eb3a6520af53c1cbf/src/code/random_scale_u16.c) scales the return of `func_80031964`; this identifies a random-draw call path but does not establish the generator's state, uniqueness, or that all randomness passes through it. Do not seed or count independent games from this function alone.

Further source trace: the matched [state setter](https://github.com/TheGh0stShip/Madden-64/blob/3f79881ce369abbba504c87eb3a6520af53c1cbf/src/code/random_state.c) writes a 32-bit value at `0x80087998`; the adjacent matched [sequence advance](https://github.com/TheGh0stShip/Madden-64/blob/3f79881ce369abbba504c87eb3a6520af53c1cbf/src/code/runtime_sequence_advance.c) updates that word and returns 16 bits. The manifest places its start at `0x80031964`, the function called by the scaling routine. This is a concrete candidate seed and an actionable live trace point, not proof that it is the sole generator or that state writes after loading produce independent game samples. Confirm reads and state changes at runtime, then replay and compare complete event sequences with and without interventions.

The C-match [event preparation routine](https://github.com/TheGh0stShip/Madden-64/blob/3f79881ce369abbba504c87eb3a6520af53c1cbf/src/code/gameplay_event_prepare.c) is a more specific trace lead: it iterates team-indexed records containing slot, type and signed value, updates player-indexed statistics, and accumulates one set of 23 team halfwords into another before calling a further routine. Its matching manifest offset `0x26710` and resident image `vram_delta` `0x7ffff400` give linked address `0x80025b10`; confirm the loaded code before installing a hook. The declared event array has capacity 30. Its event types, trigger timing and whether a frame-level reader can miss transient entries remain unknown. Capture the routine's execution or preserve intermediate states; do not assume these records are individual plays or expose complete football events. The code's separate staging and accumulation also means an instantaneous value may differ from the displayed cumulative box score.

## Candidate NFL reference sources

The NFL publishes 1996 regular-season team tables for [passing](https://www.nfl.com/stats/team-stats/offense/passing/1996/reg/all), [rushing](https://www.nfl.com/stats/team-stats/offense/rushing/1996/reg/all), [downs](https://www.nfl.com/stats/team-stats/offense/downs/1996/reg/all), and [scoring](https://www.nfl.com/stats/team-stats/offense/scoring/1996/reg/all). The downs table includes third- and fourth-down attempts and conversions as well as scrimmage plays. The passing table distinguishes gross passing yards, sacks and sack yards; do not compare gross passing yards with a net-yardage counter. The scoring table includes total team touchdowns in addition to rushing and receiving touchdowns, so distinguish offensive scoring from all team scoring. These team tables are candidate sources for matchup selection and component totals, subject to coverage, definitions and reconciliation. The 2025 Phase 7 play count explicitly includes no-play fouls; the NFL downs table is labeled scrimmage plays, so those counts cannot be directly equated without a definition bridge. No 1996 benchmark has been adopted or computed here yet.


## First end-of-game and counter cross-check

An unattended five-minute-quarter Seattle at Green Bay exhibition reached the end. At 0:00 the final Green Bay pass was shown incomplete, the scoreboard read Seattle 6, Green Bay 2, and the end-of-game message followed. In the uninterrupted run the game then returned directly to the main menu; the reason and whether another mode retains a postgame screen are unverified. A replay from `late.state` paused after that incomplete pass at 0:00, allowing Statistics → Game Stats to be displayed. The scoring summary earlier showed a Green Bay safety and Seattle field goals of 48 and 38 yards. This one game is a feasibility check, not a distribution estimate.

The box screen lists Green Bay first and Seattle second. At the paused end state it displays respectively: score 2/6; possession 9:18/10:42; first downs 7/3; offensive passing yards 172/63; rushing yards 7/19; total offense 179/82; penalties 0/0; special-teams yards 129/71; total yards 308/153; third downs 11/8, conversions 2/0; fourth downs 1/0 and conversions 0/0; turnovers 1/2. The displayed offensive and defensive red-zone counts and touchdowns are zero. These are transcribed game display values and may use game-specific definitions.

At the same paused screen, a raw RDRAM snapshot gives the following big-endian unsigned halfwords at `0x82d22 + team * 0x214c + index * 2`: indices 0 total offense, 1 offensive passing, 2 rushing, 3 turnovers, 4 third-down attempts, 5 third-down conversions, 6 fourth-down attempts, 7 fourth-down conversions, 10 total yards, 11 possession seconds, 12 first downs, and 57 score. Team index 0 is Green Bay and index 1 is Seattle in this run. Each of these values exactly matches the screen above, including 558/642 seconds for 9:18/10:42. The association is a one-game, one-state cross-check, not yet proof of semantics in other modes, before accumulation, or over a full game. The source accessor's special cases and staging must be respected. RNG candidate `0x87998` was `ad4f6245` in this snapshot; no seed or replay test has yet been performed.
