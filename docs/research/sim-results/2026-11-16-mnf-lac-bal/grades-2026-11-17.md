## Method

For each valid_complete boxscore, I compared simulated LAC points with BAL points to determine whether the actual winner, LAC, won. Win rate is LAC wins divided by valid completed games; ties count as non-wins. Total-points error is abs((simulated LAC points + simulated BAL points) - 43), and the reported MAE is the arithmetic mean across valid completed games. Blocked or errored rows are excluded from both metrics.
