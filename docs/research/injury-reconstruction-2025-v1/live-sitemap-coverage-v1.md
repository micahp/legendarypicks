# Live team-site coverage, 2025 weeks 1 to 11 (Baltimore, Buffalo, Carolina)

Status: tier 2 receipt. These are live pages found through each team's HTML article sitemap. Nothing here was checked against an archive, so none of it is cutoff-verified.

## Method

- Sitemaps fetched, one per team per month: `/sitemap/html/articles/2025/09`, `/10` and `/11` on baltimoreravens.com, buffalobills.com and panthers.com. Each was read as a list of article titles, URLs and dates.
- Matches: titles or URLs containing "injury", "injury report", "practice" (including "practice squad"), "inactives", or variants such as "injuries" and "inactive". "Injured reserve" alone was excluded.
- Dates are the sitemap's publication date, to the day. The sitemap shows no time.
- Cutoff for each game: kickoff minus 90 minutes, in UTC. The game-day inactives article is treated as covering the same cutoff.

## Caveats

- Sitemap dates may be off by a day for some articles. Buffalo's week 6 inactives is dated Oct 13 for an Oct 12 game.
- Live pages can change after publication, so these rows are unverified.
- Buffalo week 8 has no Wednesday to Friday official report in the sitemap. The Oct 24 "rules out" article covers only three players.
