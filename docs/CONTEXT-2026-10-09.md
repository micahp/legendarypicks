### Decisions

1. **Air-yards denominator is team passing air yards.** The publisher names the column
   `receiving_air_yards`, but it equals `passing_air_yards` on every team-game (2025 and 2026).
2. **Player air yards are copied from nflverse `air_yards_share`, never renormalized.** The residual is kept
   as `nfl_team_game_totals.air_yards_unassigned`.
3. **The zero-diff criterion does not apply to air yards.** Targets and carries still match exactly.
4. **Team-games where logged players exceed the published total are kept and reported, not raised.**
   This was my call on 2025-10-09, and the cause is now measured (below). It is still a rule the user
   should confirm.
5. **Team vocabulary is mapped once at the boundary** (LA to LAR, WAS to WSH).
6. **Splits rules:** an unknown teammate status excludes the game. A known contradiction puts it in
   not-matched. A teammate with no team evidence this season is not-teammate and excluded. Excluded and
   not-teammate games are counted, never silently dropped.

### Measured: what causes the air-yards overage (2025, nflverse pbp)

- Published team air yards = sum of `air_yards` over `pass_attempt == 1` plays: **544 of 544 exact.**
- **86 of 544 team-games** have logged player air yards above the published total. The excess sums to 513 yards.
- In **all 86**, the excess equals minus the sum of `air_yards` on pass plays with **no receiver_player_id**
  (161 incomplete, 1 interception). Those plays carry negative air yards (throwaways and similar). **86 of 86 exact.**
- Per player: every logged player with an `nfl_gsis_id` agrees with the pbp receiver air yards exactly.
  No logged row in an overage game lacks a player id.
- Conclusion: logged receivers are correct. The published team total includes negative-air pass plays that
  no player row can hold. The residual bucket is the right home for them, so keep-and-report is correct.
- Not yet checked: the 24 overage team-games in 2026. The 2026 pbp file is not downloaded. The same cause
  is expected but unverified.
