## 1. The news engine had stopped, and here is why

Micah opened with "why does it seem like things kind of stopped." Four
independent causes, all measured:

1. **No new conversation since 2026-08-12.** All 12 cards traced to that day.
   Discovery was working fine — 18 candidates including "Clippers Penalized"
   (52.0) and "Messi Retires Internationally" (24.0) — but nothing publishes
   without a human `--approve`, and nobody ever had.
2. **A latent trap.** `news_conversations` was EMPTY on prod, so
   `load_conversations()` was falling back to the hardcoded list. It returns DB
   rows whenever *any* exist, so approving one candidate would have collapsed the
   board from 12 cards to 1. `--sync-conversations` has to run first.
3. **96% of collected items never reached a card.** `route_conversations.py` was
   written 08-13 to place items the seed tagger cannot, and was never added to
   `news-collect.sh`. `nba-kawhi-cap` took ONE item in ten days while thirteen
   obvious Kawhi/Clippers items sat untagged.
4. **The X lane had been dead since 08-20** — 86 consecutive runs collecting
   zero and exiting 0, because every nitter mirror died in the week of X Corp's
   Aug 24 cease-and-desist.

### Fixed (`ccfc55e`)

- `route_conversations.py --apply` wired between collection and narratives.
  Tagged coverage over 10 days went **4% -> 8.2% prod, 11.4% dev**. The Kawhi
  pool went from 1 item to 50.
- Synced conversations into prod first, then approved 3 new topics and rejected
  5 duplicates (rejections are the negative labels that sharpen discovery).
  Board: **12 -> 15 prod, 16 dev**.
- Widened the `nba-kawhi-cap` seed to reach the ruling phase of the story.
- The x-only path now **exits 1** on an empty lane instead of reporting OK.

The Kawhi card now reads *"NBA suspends Clippers owner Steve Ballmer a year,
fines team $30M and strips five first-round picks"* instead of "new allegations
emerge" off an 11-day-stale pool.

**Still firing:** 3 cards speak for fans with no post in the pool, 2 cards ignore
their own publisher items. Generator quality flags, not pipeline breakage.
