## Chaos lesson from today (already lived, don't repeat)
- Broad `pkill -f "uvicorn sports_service"` hits BOTH prod (container, `--port 8000`, restart:unless-stopped revives it) AND dev (`--port 8095`). ALWAYS scope to `--port 8095` for dev. The "mystery respawning port-8000 service" earlier was just the prod container.
- Don't hold a long-lived curl against a restarting uvicorn — it jams graceful shutdown ("Waiting for connections to close") and blocks the next bind.
