## The NHL pick record (uncommitted, this session)

A code review of the NHL Model tab (read from GitHub, not the live site) raised six findings. All six were checked
against `dev` and all six hold. Two were worked today.

**The defect.** The pick was computed in the browser on every page load from the newest Bovada line, while the
forecast row was immutable. Two consequences. The recommendation could change between visits without the forecast
changing, and the Record tab never graded the recommendation at all: `RecordProjection` picked by
`home_win_probability >= 0.5`, a moneyline, while the card could show a puck line or a total. The record graded a
different selection from the one a reader was shown.

**What changed.**
- `backend/nhl_model_store.py`. New table `nhl_model_picks`, one immutable row per game and model, foreign keyed to
  the projection row, with update and delete triggers that abort. Columns: market, side, line, price,
  model_probability, market_probability, edge, expected_value, line_source, line_captured_at_utc,
  settlement_basis, provenance, selected_at_utc, pick_sha256.
- The pick rule moved out of the browser into `model_pick()` in the same module, ported exactly, including the
  `Number(x.toFixed(1))` comparison that rounds halves away from zero. There is now one implementation, so the card,
  the record and the stored row cannot drift apart.
- `lock_feed` freezes each pick against `latest_line_at(con, gid, now)`, the Bovada quote in force at that instant.
  `grade_pick` grades from the stored row under the `settlement_basis` it recorded. `export_locked` attaches the pick
  and its result; `export_previews` derives a display-only pick with the same function and never stores it.
- `backend/backfill_nhl_picks.py` (new). Freezes picks for lock rows that predate the record, priced from the line at
  their own lock timestamp. Dry run by default, `--apply` to write, refuses a Legendary Picks database path.
- `backend/routers/nhl_model.py`. The market snapshot is read once and passed to both the locked and preview exports.
- `components/Leagues/NhlModelTab.tsx`. The client-side `modelPick`, `getPickResult` and no-vig math are deleted, about
  90 lines. Cards and the record render `forecast.pick`. The record shows the pick label with its model probability,
  the result, and the frozen quote time and price.

**Verification, measured.**
- `backend/test_nhl_model_locks.py`: 23 tests, 6 new, all pass under `backend/venv/bin/python`. Two tests fail and
  both failed before this work started: `test_forecast_after_start_and_wrong_lock_window_fail_closed`
  (`(3,0,0) != (0,1,3)`) and `test_applied_daily_grade_run_also_refreshes_upcoming_previews` (`48 != 6`, the fixture
  still expects two games while the committed ledger holds sixteen). Both are recorded here so the next person does
  not read them as new.
- `components/Leagues/NhlModelTab.test.tsx`: 5 of 5 pass. The three tests that used to exercise the rule in the
  browser now render a pick supplied by the payload; the rule itself is tested in Python.
- End to end against a copy of `backend/data/nhl-model.dev.sqlite3`, through `locks_payload`: 48 picks across 16
  locked games, none missing. By market: 22 spread, 17 moneyline, 9 total. 36 graded, 28 won and 8 lost. Inserting a
  line captured in 2099 moved 0 picks.
- **9 of the 22 graded non-moneyline picks have a result that differs from the moneyline the old record graded.**
  The record was answering a different question from the card about 41 percent of the time on those picks. That is
  the number this work changes.
- The live endpoint answers on `:3096` and `:8096` and serves the picks.

**One defect introduced and corrected the same session.** The first cut also re-priced already-locked rows, so a
lock run could freeze a pick from a line posted after the forecast locked. The installed lock timer (every 15
minutes) picked that up within minutes and wrote 9 such rows for three games locked between 01:15 and 17:15 UTC,
priced from an 08:40 UTC line. The branch was removed, so `lock_feed` now freezes only the row it locks in that run
and leaves legacy rows to the backfill. The 9 rows were then deleted and rebuilt from their own lock-time line. The
signature used to find them is `selected_at_utc != locked_at_utc`. After the correction: 48 rows, every row with
`selected_at == locked_at`, both triggers present, immutability confirmed enforced.

## Still open from the review (items 3 to 6)

1. **T-90 lock policy.** `lock_feed` locks immediately and only rejects a forecast at or after puck drop. The module
   docstring, `lock_nhl_models.py`, the tab title, the tab copy and a test all assert a 90-minute window, and the repo
   ops file asserts a one-minute window on top of that. `report["missed_window"]` is never incremented, so the
   command line always reports zero missed. Decide T-90 or immediate, then make the code, the CLI, the UI, the test
   and the unit file agree.
2. **xGF/60 mislabel.** `roll_xg()` in `research/nhl/prospective-2026-27/run_pipeline.py` is a per-game mean over the
   last ten games. The Team ratings table labels it `xGF/60` and the caption says per 60. Relabel or convert.
3. **Settlement basis.** The frozen pick records `official_final_score`, the same basis the previous code used. Whether
   Bovada settles totals and puck lines on the official final score, which includes the shootout-deciding goal, is
   still unverified. The column exists so a change of basis is visible instead of rewriting past results.
4. **MoneyPuck commercial permission.** Still unresolved for a public-facing product.

## Process notes

- The pick rule now lives in one place. Two implementations of it, one in the browser and one on the server, is the
  shape of the defect this session fixed. Do not reintroduce a client-side copy for previews or anything else.

  were wrong, and only a check against `locked_at_utc` found them. Check provenance, not just presence.
