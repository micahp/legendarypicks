## 3. What was built

### Broad tape (`prediction-market-trading/broad_tape_tennis.sh`, cron `*/5`)
One `collect_orderbook.py` process holding **all** open ATP/WTA tickers in a
`[-9h, +2h]` window. Idempotent via `flock` + a `pgrep` guard on the `BROADTAPE` marker,
which rides in the `--out` path (**`collect_orderbook.py` has no `--label` flag** — the
first version used one and would have crashed on launch).

Kept separate from `watch_live.py` **on purpose**: `watch_live` spawns two processes per
event, and ~30 US Open events would be ~60 processes × 31MB against ~1GB free RAM. It
would OOM the box. Tape is the irreplaceable half — an unrecorded book is gone forever,
a signal can be recomputed from tape later — so tape goes wide and cheap in one process,
and the expensive strategy runners stay gated.

## 5. The model, and why it is shaped this way

`buy-only markdown fade v1`. Four components, all from the tape, never from a publisher:

| Component | Weight | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| `drop` | 0.32 | How far below its 25-min trailing high is this side? No markdown, no trade. |
| `band` | 0.24 | Is the price in the realised entry band? Plateau 0.06–0.35, tapering to 0.02/0.60. |
| `support` | 0.28 | Resting bid depth vs the other side. **A markdown into an empty book is a repricing, not an overreaction** — this is the component that separates them. |
| `exhaust` | 0.16 | Has the sell flow that caused the markdown stopped? The edge is fading the *end* of it. |

Deliberately not modelled: momentum/continuation (standing rule is fade, never chase) and
any short side. One card per event — both sides of a two-way market are the same trade
seen twice.

First run: 162 markets on tape, 14 candidates. Top was
`KXATPMATCH-26SEP02ETCFEA-FEA` at 0.27 off a 0.50 trailing high, 46% depth support.

**Unvalidated.** The weights are reasoned from the fills, not fitted. The tape accumulating
tonight is the first dataset that can actually backtest them — which is the point: the old
gate only ever let 6 of 31 WTA events through, so any model fitted before today was fitted
on a biased sample.

# APPENDED 18:5x — the causal layer landed

## 9. Live match state: we can now say WHY a price moved

The tape records that a price moved. It cannot say a player was just broken, which is what
decides whether the move is reversible — the entire stop-loss / take-profit question. Two
feeds now supply that, both free, neither ESPN.

### Official US Open point-by-point (the big one)

```
config      /en_US/json/gen/config_web.json        <- declares all 35 feed paths
live        /en_US/scores/feeds/2026/matches/live/scores.json
points      /en_US/scores/feeds/2026/slamtracker/history/<matchId>C.json
```

IBM SlamTracker's own data. **57 fields per point**: `BreakPoint` / `BreakPointWon` /
`BreakPointOpportunity`, IBM's own `P1Momentum` / `P2Momentum`, the 0/15/30/40 score inside
the game, ace, double fault, unforced error, winner shot type, serve speed and placement,
rally count, distance run, and a plain-English `Sentence`.

**`EpochTimeStart` is the load-bearing field** — it joins a point to the order book by time,
turning "price fell 15c at 18:32" into "he was broken at 18:31".

First run: **1,248 points across 10 live matches, 23 break points won.** Cron every minute
(`usopen_points.py`).

**How it was found, because the method is the lesson:** five guessed URLs 404'd and the
earlier audit wrote it up as "unresolved, needs a headless browser". No browser was needed.
Page HTML -> `src=` -> JS bundle -> grep -> one config file that declares every path. The
real path was `matches/live/scores.json`, one segment off every guess. A guessed URL that
404s means you guessed wrong, not that the data is absent. Encoded in the
`answer-is-already-here` skill, new §2b.

**Scope limit:** tournament-scoped. Works for the US Open, stops when it ends.

### Bovada — the general fallback, and the ESPN pullback

`/services/sports/results/api/v1/scores/<eventId>` with
`Accept: application/vnd.scoreboards.full+json` (it 406s on `application/json`, and the 406
body lists what it wants — the error is the documentation).

Gives sets, games, and **server**, which makes completed breaks computable. 33 live tennis
events plus soccer, 104 captured per pass, cron every minute (`bovada_scores.py`).
**No point score** — that is the line between it and the US Open feed.

Why it matters: ESPN declares `statsSource: none` for tennis AND both its hosts were 403ing
under the burst limit — a budget shared with the production scoreboard ingest, so every
probe spent there is one prod cannot make.

## 10. Why the US Open specifically

Men's singles is **best-of-five**. A player two sets down is not finished, so a market
pricing them as finished is fadeable in a way a best-of-three never is. Observed live:
Molcan 1-2 down entering set 4. Break points and set points are the momentum hinges, and
both are now detectable with IBM's own momentum number attached.
