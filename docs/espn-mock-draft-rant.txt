The ESPN Mock Draft System Is Pure Garbage
The year is 2022 and doing mock drafts on ESPN feel about the same as they did when I started playing in 2012. While I love doing mocks on Sleeper, a couple of my drafts are on ESPN every year, so I try to feel out the ADPs and the draft board in the ESPN mocks. The difference between the platforms is night and day. While I understand that Sleeper can offer more control in settings because you can draft in your own room, ESPN offers one of the least user friendly experiences I've endured in fantasy football and needs serious work. I'm sure I'm leaving some things off, but here are some of my biggest complaints:

The mock draft lobby. I don’t even know where to begin here. When you go to start your mock draft on ESPN, you get taken to what I can only describe as the Public Health Department waiting room of fantasy football. You get welcomed with a list of “Rooms Drafting Soon” that seem to be in no clear order, just a mix of “Beginner”, “Pro”, and “Expert” 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, and 20 team PPR and non snake or salary cap draft. If you’re looking for a draft that matches your league, good luck. There’s no option to filter for any of this. You just have to look through each line and once you find what you are looking for, you have to pray that it has not filled up in the time it takes for you to load in. This time of year it always fills up. No worries though, you can just click find another mock draft and be put into an empty lobby that will be waiting at least an additional 5 minutes! (I will touch on that later).

Beginner, Pro, and Expert drafts. Seriously wtf is this? Does anybody care what “level” of draft they’re in? I’m a junkie, we’re all junkies here, so I’m sure we’d all consider ourselves “experts.” If I joined an “Expert” draft, I’m not expecting anything other than 11 year olds. Hell, if I join any of these drafts, I’m expecting 11 year olds and will be pleasantly surprised when I’m in a room with people who know what they’re doing and stay beyond 6 rounds. When I went to look at the descriptions of the rooms for the exact details on this, I saw a “Beginner 20 Team” room. I can’t imagine what kind of psychopath joins a 20 team league and does their mocks on ESPN, but if you’re a beginner in a 20 team league, God bless you.

Where is my Half Point? Notice in point one I said they had PPR and Non options. They don’t have an option for half point PPR. In the year of our lord 2022.

The wait. Remember when I said “Rooms Drafting soon?” If you want to casually do a mock draft when you have some downtime, it will take longer than you think. These rooms run on 5 minute intervals, so you can’t just hop on and draft. The good news is you have plenty of time to prepare your materials because even when you do get into a room that’s about to draft, there’s still 3 minutes before the draft actually begins. So this means you can be waiting at times up to 8 minutes between deciding to draft and the draft actually starting. I have no doubt in my mind this leads to people forgetting they’re in the room and auto-drafting the entire thing.

The draft board. I noticed recently that they don’t have the boards in order of ADP on default but rather in order of the ESPN fantasy rankings. This isn’t the biggest deal and you can sort by ADP manually, but it just feels strange and likely influences the casuals.

Your results. Got your draft coming up and want to look back at how your teams shaped up? Good news! You can look at your results in the email they send you once the draft is over. The bad news is, you can't see all of your picks in one spot. You can't see your team view. The email is just a running list of picks in the order they occurred. Sure it is nice to see the full order, but at least offer both and give me a place I can see my picks all in one spot. Hell, even just a picture of a fake draft board would be amazing as it would let me see all my picks down a single line and the full order.

Honestly, part of me is hoping as I post this that something is wrong on my end. That one of if not the biggest platforms for fantasy actually offers a great experience and I am missing something. Otherwise, I hope this gets updated next year.
