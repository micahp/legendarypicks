## NHL: the projection was degenerate, and the score model is replaced

The model's stored projections had a margin sd of **0.50 goals against an observed 2.39**, and a
total sd of **0.18 against 2.08**. 54% of 105 locked games projected a margin under half a goal.
Inputs were not the problem in scale (Elo diff sd 90.2, log(xgf−xga) sd 0.203) — the fitted
coefficients (0.24–0.30) capped the output at roughly ±1.5 goals. Every game looked like a coin
flip, so the leeway pick rule fired on about half the slate.

`research/nhl/prospective-2026-27/team_strength.py` + `frozen_models_2026-27_v2.json`, branch
`research/nhl-team-strength-2026-10-19` @ **`4ad7b0ba`**: a ridge Poisson over team attack,
defence, home advantage and neutral-site suppression, 400-day recency half-life, fitted on the
canonical file.

- Walk-forward Brier **0.2436 vs 0.2463** for v1; wins five of six seasons, loses 2025-26
- Margin sd **0.65 vs 0.25**
- Slate effect: ANA @ NYR +0.17 → +0.84, SJS @ TOR +0.28 → +1.14

**Not an edge.** The closest matched comparison (goals+xG blend on 5,660 games overlapping SBR) is
0.2445 against the market's 0.2395.

The wiring does not run yet: `run_pipeline.py` pins `…-v1` at lines 239 and 474, the v2 artifact
